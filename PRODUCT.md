# Bloom

## Register

product

## Users

Bloom is for Indian women aged 18 to 35 who live with PCOS, suspected PCOS, or cycles that do not follow a predictable pattern. They are often checking in during a short work or study break, may share their device with family, and need language that feels private, culturally fluent, and free of fertility pressure.

The primary job is to notice and understand body patterns in under a minute without feeling judged, diagnosed, or pushed toward weight loss or pregnancy.

## Product Purpose

Bloom is a privacy-first daily body companion. It helps users record mood, energy, sleep, pain, flow, and symptoms; view their unique cycle history; discover gentle observational patterns; learn from culturally relevant health content; and prepare useful summaries for healthcare visits.

Success means the user leaves each session feeling more understood and more in control of her data. The core daily loop should take less than 60 seconds, with the check-in itself taking less than 30 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, and quietly encouraging.

Bloom speaks like a thoughtful friend with strong boundaries. It validates before it suggests, uses plain language, and never treats a varied cycle as a failure. It is emotionally intelligent without becoming sentimental, and medically responsible without becoming clinical.

## Anti-references

- Cold hospital portals, clinical dashboards, or diagnostic red-alert interfaces
- Fertility-first period trackers that frame cycle variation as late, missed, or abnormal
- Gym, calorie-counting, or weight-loss aesthetics
- Overly decorative wellness apps with beige-on-beige contrast, mystical claims, or vague self-care copy
- Dense spreadsheets, gamified streak pressure, guilt-driven reminders, and confetti for routine health data
- Generic AI gradients, glassmorphism, floating cards everywhere, and emoji-heavy navigation

## Design Principles

1. Make understanding the reward. Every interaction should clarify a pattern or validate an experience.
2. Invite, never pressure. Logging, reminders, and empty states should make returning feel easy and guilt-free.
3. Privacy must be visible. Local storage, app locking, export, and deletion should be explained in plain language at the point of need.
4. One glance, one next step. Each primary screen should reveal the user’s current context and the most useful action without scanning.
5. Indian context is product depth. Language, food, remedies, and healthcare guidance should feel native rather than translated in later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast with at least 4.5:1 for body text
- Minimum 44 by 44 point touch targets, with 48 points preferred
- Dynamic text and screen-reader labels for every interactive control
- No status communicated by color alone
- Reduced-motion support and no decorative motion that blocks the task
- English plus Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, and Marathi structures
- Plain-language medical disclaimers and observational, non-diagnostic insight copy
